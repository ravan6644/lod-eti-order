# Product Requirement Document: LOD DELICACY Digital Ordering Platform

## 1. Introduction
This document outlines the requirements for building a digital ordering platform for LOD DELICACY. The primary goals are to increase sales, streamline operations, provide a seamless mobile ordering experience, reduce wait times at the physical location, and build customer loyalty through a digital rewards program.

## 2. Product Vision
To provide a seamless mobile ordering experience for customers in the Eti-Osa area, reduce wait times at the physical location, and build customer loyalty through a digital rewards program.

## 3. User Personas
*   **Busy Professionals:** Seeking quick lunch options.
*   **Local Residents:** Ordering dinner for pickup or delivery.
*   **Restaurant Staff:** Managing incoming orders and inventory.

## 4. Functional Requirements
### 4.1 Digital Menu
*   Display all food items with descriptions, prices, and high-quality images.
*   Should be easily updatable by restaurant staff.

### 4.2 Order Management
*   Allow users to select items and customize their orders.
*   Implement a digital shopping cart functionality.
*   Process orders efficiently for pickup or delivery.

### 4.3 Payment Integration
*   Support local payment methods, including bank transfers and card payments.
*   Ensure secure and reliable payment processing.

### 4.4 Real-Time Tracking
*   Provide customers with status updates from order confirmation to pickup or delivery.
*   Integrate with delivery logistics if applicable.

### 4.5 Loyalty System
*   Award points for every purchase to encourage repeat business.
*   Define rules for point redemption and reward tiers.

## 5. Technical Specifications
*   Develop a responsive web application compatible with iOS and Android devices.
*   Integrate a backend system to manage orders, users, and menu availability in real-time.
*   Utilize secure APIs for payment processing and user data protection.
*   Consider scalability for future growth.

## 6. Success Metrics
*   Increase total monthly orders by 20% within six months.
*   Maintain an average order fulfillment time of less than 30 minutes.
*   Achieve a 15% adoption rate for the loyalty program in the first quarter.

## 7. Prioritization Discussion
This document includes requirements for both delivery logistics and customer loyalty features. Both are critical for the platform's success. For the initial phase, we will ensure a robust foundation for both. Further refinement on the depth of delivery logistics vs. loyalty features can be discussed based on immediate business priorities.
